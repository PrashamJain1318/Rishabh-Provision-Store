# 📖 Rishabh Provision Store - Customer User Guide

Welcome to Rishabh Provision Store Online Shopping Portal!

## 🛍️ How to Place an Order
1. Browse products or search by category (*Atta, Rice, Oil, Spices, Dairy*).
2. Click **Add to Cart** on desired grocery items.
3. Open your shopping cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**.
4. Select or add your delivery address (*Home / Office*).
5. Apply promotional discount coupons (*e.g., FIRST50 or DIWALI20*).
6. Select your payment method (*UPI QR Code, Cash on Delivery, Wallet*).
7. Track your order timeline in real-time from **My Account $\rightarrow$ Orders**.
